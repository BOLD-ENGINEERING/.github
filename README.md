# BOLD ENGINEERING

> Engineering at the edge of autonomy, defense, and high-performance automation.

## What We Do
Bold Engineering is dedicated to building robust, tactical-grade technology. Our work spans from autonomous systems and defense tech to the development of modular, high-utility software frameworks. We believe in engineering solutions that are as resilient as they are efficient.

### Core Focus Areas
*   **Tactical Systems:** Research and development of autonomous and defense-oriented technologies.
*   **Modular Automation:** Building scalable frameworks (Bash, Laravel-inspired structures) to unionize complex scripting and system tasks.
*   **High-Performance Web & CLI:** Leveraging Next.js and self-documenting CLI tools to create seamless interfaces for complex backend operations.
*   **Infrastructure & Self-Hosting:** Advancing the "Lab" environment through Proxmox, Docker, and specialized Linux configurations.
